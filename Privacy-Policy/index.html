02

Data We Collect

🛡️
Zero Data Collection Policy
HaiMail does not collect any personal data. No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identifiers are collected or stored by us. We are committed to a privacy-first, zero-collection approach.

Usage Data

Usage Data may be collected automatically by the underlying infrastructure or third-party platforms (such as the app store). This may include device type, operating system version, and diagnostic data. This data is not personally identifiable and is not collected directly by HaiMail.

03

How We Use Your Data

Since HaiMail does not collect personal data, we do not use, sell, share, or process any personal information about You. Any usage statistics made available through third-party platforms (such as app stores) are used solely to improve the Service.

04

Retention Periods

As HaiMail does not collect personal data, no retention periods are applicable to personal information. Any anonymized or aggregated data made available through third-party platforms is governed by those platforms' own retention policies.

05

Data Transfers

HaiMail does not transfer any personal data internationally, as no personal data is collected. If this changes in the future, We will update this Privacy Policy and ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

06

Your Rights & Deletion

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data We may hold about You. Since HaiMail does not collect personal data, there is generally no data to access or delete.

However, You may contact Us at any time with such requests and We will respond promptly. If You have an Account, You may manage Your information by visiting the account settings section of the application.

08

Security

The security of Your information is important to Us. While We strive to use commercially reasonable means to protect any data associated with the Service, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

09

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under 16. If You are a parent or guardian and believe Your child has provided Us with any data, please contact Us immediately.

If We become aware that We have inadvertently collected data from a child under 16, We will take immediate steps to remove that information from Our systems.

10

Third-Party Links

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites not operated by Us. If You click on a third-party link, You will be directed to that site. We strongly advise You to review the Privacy Policy of every site You visit.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the Last updated date at the top.

Where changes are significant, We will notify You via email or a prominent notice within the Service prior to the change becoming effective.
