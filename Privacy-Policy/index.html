02

Data We Collect

🛡️ HaiMail does not collect any personal data. We are committed to a privacy-first approach — no names, emails, phone numbers, or identifiers are collected or stored by us.

Usage Data

Usage Data may be collected automatically by the underlying infrastructure or third-party tools (such as the app store platform) and may include device type, operating system version, and diagnostic data. This data is not personally identifiable and is not collected directly by HaiMail.

03

How We Use Your Data

Since HaiMail does not collect personal data, we do not use, sell, share, or process any personal information about You. Any usage statistics made available through third-party platforms (such as app stores) are used solely to improve the Service.

04

Retention Periods

As HaiMail does not collect personal data, there are no retention periods applicable to personal information. Any anonymized or aggregated data made available through third-party platforms is governed by those platforms' own retention policies.

HaiMail does not transfer any personal data internationally, as no personal data is collected.
